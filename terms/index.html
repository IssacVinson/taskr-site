2. What Taskr Is

Taskr is an online marketplace that connects:

  • Seekers — people who post tasks (listings) for local help; and
  • Taskrs — people who book and perform those tasks.

Taskr is a platform, not the employer, contractor, or agent of Seekers or Taskrs (except as needed to operate payments/escrow as described below). Seekers and Taskrs contract with each other for the task; Taskr provides tools, messaging, and payment facilitation.

Listings use one of two price types:

  • Fixed price: the Seeker sets the price, and the task is booked at that price.
  • Negotiable: the listing has no set price. The person booking the task enters their price and pays it when they book, and that money is held in escrow until the job is done (see Section 5). The Seeker does not accept or approve the price first.

4. Listings, booking, and address privacy

5. Payments, escrow, platform fee, and tips

Payments are processed by Stripe (including Stripe Connect for Taskr payouts). By using paid features you also agree to applicable Stripe terms.

5.1 Escrow and split (platform fee)

  1. When a task is booked and paid for (at the fixed price, or at the price entered when booking a negotiable task), funds are typically authorized/captured into escrow held via Stripe until release conditions are met.
  2. On successful completion and release, the task price is split approximately:
    • ~90% to the Taskr (payout via Connect, subject to Stripe readiness and verification); and
    • ~10% platform fee to Arctic Autonomy Ventures LLC d/b/a Taskr.
  3. Exact amounts, timing, and currency are shown in-app. Taxes (if any) are your responsibility unless we state otherwise in writing.

5.2 Dual confirmation and release

  1. Completion generally requires dual confirmation (Seeker and Taskr confirm the work is done), or other release rules we publish in-app.
  2. After release, escrow is paid out per the split above.
  3. Auto-release or time-based release may apply if described in-app; we may adjust those rules with notice.

5.3 Tips

  1. After escrow release, Seekers may optionally tip the Taskr.
  2. Tips are paid 100% to the Taskr (platform fee 0% on tips), subject to Stripe processing and Taskr Connect payout readiness.
  3. Tips are voluntary and generally non-refundable once paid, except as required by law or a successful dispute determination.

5.4 Failed or incomplete payouts

If a Taskr’s Connect account cannot receive transfers (for example payouts not ready), payment may fail or be delayed. We may show an in-app error and ask the Taskr to finish onboarding.
