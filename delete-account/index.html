Delete your Taskr account

This page explains how to delete your account in the Taskr app, developed by Arctic Autonomy Ventures LLC, and what happens to your data.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open the Taskr app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Review what will be deleted and what will be kept, then confirm. You may be asked to enter a one-time code sent to your email to confirm it's you.

You'll be signed out and won't be able to sign in to the deleted account again.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't use the app, email support@taskrak.com:

  • Send it from the email address on your Taskr account, so we can confirm the request is yours.
  • Use the subject line "Delete my Taskr account".

We may reply to confirm before we delete anything. We won't ask for your card number or password.

You can always delete your account, even if it is banned or you have bookings in progress. Here's what happens to your bookings when you delete (see Terms Section 8):

  • Work hasn't started: the booking is cancelled and the Seeker gets a full refund, with no late fee, no matter which side deleted.
  • Work has started but isn't confirmed: the payment stays frozen until Taskr support decides whether to refund the Seeker or release the payment to the Taskr.
  • Finished and confirmed: the payment is released to the Taskr as usual.

Requests still waiting for payment end, and no one is charged. If you're a Seeker, your listings close. If you're a Taskr, any listing you were booked on goes back on the board as Open.

What we delete

These are deleted right away when you delete your account, and removed from our backups within 30 days:

  • Your display name, email address, home ZIP code, and listed skills. Your profile is replaced with "Deleted user."
  • Your photos, and the addresses on your tasks.
  • Your draft and open listings, which are cancelled.
  • Blocks you set.
  • Your ability to sign in, and your sign-in codes (which expire after 10 minutes anyway). You can't sign in to a deleted account again.

If you set up payouts, your identity and bank details are held by Stripe, not Taskr. Payouts for finished jobs still go to your own Stripe account, and you can still reach that account through Stripe after you delete your Taskr account. Stripe keeps its own records of your Connect account under Stripe's privacy policy.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Booking, payment, and dispute records (for example amounts, platform fees, payouts, tips, refunds, cancellations, confirmations, and dispute outcomes), which we must keep for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, and dispute purposes. We keep these for up to 7 years, with your identity removed.
  • Ratings and booking chat messages that are part of those records may stay attached to the booking, shown as from "Deleted user."
  • Reports you filed, kept with your identity removed for safety, moderation, and legal reasons, on the same retention terms as the records above. Reports other people filed about you may be kept for moderation.
  • Information we must keep to comply with a legal obligation or to resolve an open legal claim, only for as long as that requires.

Timeline

Your personal details are deleted right away when you delete your account, and removed from our backups within 30 days. Booking and payment records are kept with your identity removed, and are deleted at the end of their retention period (up to 7 years).

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Edit or clear your display name, home ZIP, and skills in Profile.
  • Unblock users in Profile.
  • Cancel your draft or open listings in the app to remove them and their photos.
  • Turn off location access in your device settings at any time.
  • For anything else, email support@taskrak.com from your account email.
